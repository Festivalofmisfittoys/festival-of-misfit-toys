🎸 Festival of Misfit Toys 🎄

The Festival

Festival of Misfit Toys is a free, all-ages metal festival bringing together Florida's independent heavy music community while collecting toys for local children during the holiday season.

Hosted at Bay Street Yard in Downtown Fort Myers, the festival features two stages, more than ten Florida-based bands, vendors, food, drinks, merchandise, and much more.

Bring a new, unwrapped toy and support local children this holiday season.

The Festival

Festival of Misfit Toys is a free, all-ages metal festival dedicated to supporting local children during the holiday season.

Hosted at Bay Street Yard in Downtown Fort Myers, the festival features two stages, 10+ independent Florida bands, vendors, merchandise, food, drinks, and much more.

Bring a new, unwrapped toy and help make the holidays brighter.

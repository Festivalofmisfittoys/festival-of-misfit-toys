More Than A Festival

Festival of Misfit Toys is a free, all-ages metal festival bringing together Florida's independent music scene while supporting local children during the holiday season.

Enjoy two stages, 10+ Florida bands, vendors, merchandise, food, drinks, and a community toy drive in the heart of Downtown Fort Myers.

🎸 Two Stages

Multiple stages featuring Florida heavy music.

🤘 10+ Florida Bands

Independent artists from across the state.

🧸 Community Toy Drive

Bring a toy and help local children during the holidays.

🛍 Vendors & Merch

Festival merch, artists, and local vendors.

🍔 Food & Drinks

Food, beverages, and refreshments available throughout the day.

Bring A Toy, Not A Ticket

Admission is completely free.

Instead of purchasing a ticket, attendees are encouraged to bring a new, unwrapped toy donation to help brighten the holiday season for local children.

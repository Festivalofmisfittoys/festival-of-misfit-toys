Festival of Misfit Toys

A Free Metal Festival & Community Toy Drive

Saturday, November 28

Bay Street Yard • Downtown Fort Myers

Bring a New, Unwrapped Toy Instead of Purchasing a Ticket

Heavy Music. Big Hearts.

Festival of Misfit Toys is a free, all-ages metal festival bringing together Florida's independent music scene to support local children during the holiday season.

Hosted at Bay Street Yard in Downtown Fort Myers, the festival features two stages, 10+ Florida bands, vendors, food, drinks, merchandise, and a community toy drive.

The full lineup will be announced September 8.

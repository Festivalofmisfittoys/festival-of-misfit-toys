GOOD TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is the festival all ages?

Yes! Festival of Misfit Toys is an all-ages event.

How much does it cost?

Admission is completely free.

Do I need to bring a toy?

No, but toy donations are strongly encouraged.

Can I bring a chair?

No. Outside chairs are not permitted. There will be seating throughout the venue.

Is the event rain or shine?

Yes. The festival is rain or shine.

Can I bring a camera and take photos?

Yes. We do not have backstage or media passes at this time. Tag us on instagram!

FAQ

Everything You Need To Know

IS THE FESTIVAL ALL AGES?

Yes! Festival of Misfit Toys is an all-ages event.

HOW MUCH DOES IT COST?

Admission is completely free.

Instead of purchasing a ticket, attendees are encouraged to bring a new, unwrapped toy donation.

DO I NEED TO BRING A TOY?

No. Donations are voluntary, but they are strongly encouraged to help local children during the holiday season.

CAN I BRING A CHAIR?

No. Outside chairs are not permitted.

Seating is available throughout Bay Street Yard.

IS THE FESTIVAL RAIN OR SHINE?

Yes. Festival of Misfit Toys will take place rain or shine.

WHEN WILL THE LINEUP BE ANNOUNCED?

The full lineup will be revealed on September 8.

WILL THERE BE FOOD AND DRINKS?

Yes. Food and beverages will be available throughout the event.

WILL THERE BE VENDORS AND MERCH?

Yes. Festival merchandise, artists, and local vendors will be on site.

ARE BAND APPLICATIONS STILL OPEN?

No. Applications for this year's event have closed.

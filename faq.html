Frequently Asked Questions

Is the festival all ages?

Yes! Festival of Misfit Toys is an all-ages event.

How much does it cost?

Admission is completely free.

Do I need to bring a toy?

No, but attendees are encouraged to bring a new, unwrapped toy to support local children during the holiday season.

Can I bring a chair?

No. Outside chairs are not permitted. Seating is available throughout Bay Street Yard.

Is the festival rain or shine?

Yes. Festival of Misfit Toys will take place rain or shine.

Will there be food and drinks?

Absolutely! Bay Street Yard offers food and beverages throughout the event.

Will there be vendors and merch?

Yes! Festival merchandise, band merchandise, artists, and vendors will be on site.

Are band applications still open?

No. Band applications for this year's festival have closed.

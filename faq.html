Frequently Asked Questions

Is the festival all ages?

Yes! Festival of Misfit Toys is an all-ages event.

How much does it cost?

Admission is completely free.

Do I need to bring a toy?

No, but toy donations are encouraged.

Can I bring a chair?

No. Outside chairs are not permitted.

Is the event rain or shine?

Yes. Festival of Misfit Toys is rain or shine.

Will there be food and drinks?

Yes. Food and beverages will be available throughout the event.

Will there be vendors and merch?

Yes! Festival merchandise, band merchandise, artists, and vendors will be on site.

Are band applications still open?

No. Applications for this year have closed.
